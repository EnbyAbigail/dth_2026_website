## The Disadvantages of Open Source

Open source software and operating systems are highly prevalent in technology today, given that many different companies use it and much of it is utilized even within closed source software, *Linux* is used as the OS of android phones as well as of many other pieces of hardware. Despite that, open source comes with some issues.

Open source OS like *Linux* may give the user or company in depth access to their tools, allowing them to adapt them to their needs, but it also is a **far less user-friendly experience**. Many users find the switch to *Linux* **intimidating and foreign** compared to the GUI the normally use. Organizations may also need to **train employees in the use of open source**, which can be a **financial and time strain** (Mangnani 2023; Pratt 2022).

Combining open source with proprietary closed source software or hardware may also present further challenges, especially in a corporate setting, and can be **time and energy exhaustive** (Pratt 2022; Morgan 2007). This can further be difficult when needing driver updates, as your system **may not be given as much attention and effort** (Mangnani 2023). Running into problems is common with technology, but when utilizing open source there are often **not the traditional sources of support** as there are with company owned and run closed source software and OS, which **may leave the average user stuck in a problem**.
