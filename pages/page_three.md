## Trafilatura and How it Helps Historians

As an English history, society, and culture student, archived information is key to documenting historical turns and actions. Throughout history historical documents have been lost to time or destroyed with intent, therefore preservation has become a vital practice for historians. Much information today is not stored physically, but is instead found on social media and on websites, often filled with cookies, advertisements and other obstacles to retrieving information, and those sites are often removed or changed with no reprimand.

Trafilatura is an open source tool used to extract text and essential metadata directly from websites, such as articles, which in turn makes wide website collection/scraping much simpler and easier to consume for researchers.  **Created by Adrien Barbaresi who goes by ‘adbar’ on Github**, Trafilatura has had **108 unique contributors with over 1600 commits**, most of which came from Barbaresi himself, with the **latest commit being on the 6. Of November 2026**. When issues are submitted to the Github page, they are often resolved, though some still have not been. The project itself was developed by Barbaresi independently during his studies, and has not received any outside funding, relying on volunteering contributors instead.
Trafilatura uses the **Apache License 2.0** which allows users to “modify, distribute, and sublicense the original source code” as well as use it commercially and have patent claims (Tal).

Trafilatura is usefil in my major of English with a thesis focused on history. Writing and researching nationalism and Donald Trump it would allow me to quicker and easier scrape the upwards of a hundred news articles I need for discourse analysis, only retaining essential html code and not all the clutter. It also helps solve the issue of website information periodically changing and the loss of previous data. The tool also helps retain information from sites at risk of deletion, such as government websites and datasets under the current US administration (Bennet 2025).
